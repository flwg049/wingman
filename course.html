Welcome to the Cadet Wingman Course!

In this course, you will be introduced to the Core Values, the Wingman Concept, Safety, and Cadet Protection.

Throughout this course, you will watch two videos to build your understanding of the Cadet Wingman concept. Please pay close attention to the details in each video, as the knowledge you gain will be assessed in the final quiz.

Video - Cadet Wingman Video

Learning Points

This video focuses on four main topics: Core Values, the wingman concept, safety, and cadet protection. As you watch, think about:

  • What do the Core Values mean to you? How can they shape your actions and decisions as a cadet?
  • How does the wingman concept apply to your daily life? Reflect on ways you can support and protect your fellow cadets.
  • Why is a pro-safety attitude crucial for cadets? How would you react in situations where safety might be at risk?
  • What are some warning signs of abuse to look out for in your wingman? What actions would you take if you suspect abuse?

Video - Batman Begins

Learning Points

As you watch "Batman Begins: The Final Test," reflect on these questions. There are no right or wrong answers, so think deeply about your values:

1. Honor and Moral Decisions

Why does Batman choose his moral code over executing the prisoner? How would you handle a situation where you must choose between orders and your morals?

2. Core Values in Action

How do Batman's actions show values like courage, respect, or integrity? How do your values guide your choices in tough situations?

3. The Role of Compassion

Why is compassion important when making hard decisions? How can showing compassion help you as a wingman?

4. Heroes vs. Villains

What separates a "hero" from a "villain"? How can core values define one's character?

5. Applying Core Values

How do CAP's Core Values play a role in everyday life? Recall a time you acted in line with these values. What did you learn?

Use these questions to think about how Batman's actions relate to your own values.
